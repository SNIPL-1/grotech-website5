About Us

Our Story

Established in 2012 and headquartered in Ludhiana, Punjab, India, Sri Neelkanth Impex Private Limited has emerged as a distinguished manufacturer and exporter of premium-quality tools. Over the years, we've built a reputation for excellence in delivering Hand Tools, Garden Tools, Automotive Tools, Plumbing Tools, and Machine Tools.

Our products are meticulously designed with accurate dimensions, exceptional strength, durable finishes, and corrosion resistance—making them ideal for diverse industrial applications. We pride ourselves on adhering to universally recognized quality standards.

Our state-of-the-art infrastructure, equipped with cutting-edge technology and managed by a team of seasoned professionals, enables us to efficiently meet both standard and customized requirements. From our modern quality testing laboratory to our dedicated workforce, every aspect of our operation is geared towards delivering excellence.

Under the visionary leadership of our Managing Director, Mr. Shiv Jindal, and General Manager, Mr. Keshav Jindal, we continue to grow exponentially, serving clients across the nation and beyond with ethical business practices and transparent dealings.

Our Vision

To become a global leader in tool manufacturing and exports, recognized for innovation, quality, and customer satisfaction.

Our Mission

To deliver world-class tools that empower professionals and industries worldwide. We are committed to continuous improvement, sustainable practices, and maintaining the highest standards of quality in every product we manufacture.

Quality Assurance & Global Presence

From raw material inspection to final product testing, we maintain strict quality protocols. With a strong export network, we've established ourselves as a trusted partner in the global tools industry.
